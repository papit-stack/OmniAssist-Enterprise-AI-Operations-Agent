IT AND SECURITY POLICY - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

Purpose
This policy protects company data, systems, and employees from cyber threats. All employees must read and acknowledge this policy during onboarding and annually.

Acceptable Use of Company Devices and Systems
- Company-issued laptops, phones, and accounts must be used for business purposes. Minor personal use is acceptable but should not interfere with work.
- Do not store personal or non-company confidential data on company devices beyond incidental amounts.
- Company accounts (email, VPN, project tools) are for official use. Sharing passwords is strictly prohibited.

Passwords and Authentication
- All company accounts must use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). Set up MFA on day 1 for your email and SSO accounts.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include upper case, lower case, numbers, and special characters.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Never reuse the last 5 passwords.
- In case of a suspected compromise, immediately report to security@novatech.example and change your password.

Device Management
- All devices must have the company antivirus (SentinelOne) installed and be kept updated (auto-updates enable).
- Keep your device encrypted (BitLocker/FileVault enabled by default).
- Do not install unauthorized software. Software requests go through the IT approval workflow.
- Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 2 hours; the device is remotely wiped if needed.

Data Classification and Handling
- Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
- Customer data is Confidential or Restricted. It must never leave approved systems, be emailed externally, or be copied to personal devices.
- Personal/sensitive employee information is Restricted and accessible only to HR and authorized payroll team.
- Use the official data-sharing tool (SharePoint) for sharing; do not use unauthorized file-sharing services for company data.
- Clean desk policy: lock your screen when away (Windows key + L), do not leave confidential documents unattended.

Internet and Email Security
- Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments. Report phishing to security@novatech.example.
- Company email is monitored for security compliance.
- Browsing of inappropriate or illegal content is prohibited and is a disciplinary offense.

Work from Home Security
- Connect to office networks only through the company VPN (Cisco Secure Client).
- Ensure home WiFi uses WPA2/3 encryption and a strong password.
- Do not leave laptops unattended in public places. Use laptop privacy screens when traveling.
- Print confidential documents only in the office, not on home printers.

Incident Response
- Any security incident (malware, phishing, data breach, lost device) must be reported within 4 hours to security@novatech.example.
- Attempts to bypass security controls, or knowingly exposing data, lead to disciplinary action including possible termination.

IT Security FAQ
- How do I set up MFA? Go to the identity portal, scan the QR code, and register your authenticator app.
- What is the password length requirement? Minimum 12 characters.
- Can I use my personal laptop for work? Only in exception cases with IT approval.
- Whom to contact for IT issues? ithelpdesk@novatech.example or raise a ticket on the portal.

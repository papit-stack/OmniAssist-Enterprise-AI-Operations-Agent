LEAVE POLICY - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

All leave is managed through the employee portal. Approval is required from the reporting manager. This policy applies to all permanent employees.

Types of Leave
1. Earned Leave (EL): 18 days per year. Eligible after completing 6 months of service. Can be encashed up to a maximum of 5 days per year. Unused EL of up to 30 days can be carried forward to the next year.
2. Casual Leave (CL): 8 days per year. Meant for short, unplanned absences. Cannot be carried forward. At least 1 day advance notice is expected unless it is an emergency.
3. Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per year. For illness or medical appointments. A medical certificate is required if sick leave is taken for more than 2 consecutive days. Cannot be carried forward.
4. Maternity Leave: 26 weeks (with full pay) as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Can be taken not earlier than 8 weeks before the expected delivery date. Cannot be encashed.
5. Paternity Leave: 10 days of paid leave for new fathers, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
6. Adoption Leave: 12 weeks of paid leave when adopting a child below 1 year of age.
7. Bereavement Leave: 3 paid days in the case of death of a family member (spouse, parents, children, or in-laws).
8. Marriage Leave: 3 paid days, applicable within 3 months of the marriage date.
9. Comp-off: For work performed on a public holiday or on a weekly off. 1 full day of comp-off is granted and must be availed within 30 days.
10. Public/National Holidays: 12 gazetted holidays are observed as per the official holiday calendar released every year in January.

Yearly Leave Summary
Total paid leave per year (EL + CL + SL): 36 days, excluding public holidays.

General Rules
- Leave must be requested at least 2 working days in advance, except sick or emergency leave.
- Leave is calculated on a calendar year basis (January 1 to December 31).
- Leaves lapsed at the end of the year may not be carried forward except EL, as mentioned above.
- Employees under notice period may take leave only with approval of HR and manager.

Leave FAQ
- How many days of sick leave do I get? 10 days per year.
- How many casual leaves can I take? 8 per year.
- Can I carry forward my casual leave? No, only Earned Leave up to 30 days can be carried forward.
- How do I apply for leave? Go to the Employee Portal > Leave > Apply Leave > select type and dates > submit for manager approval.
- Whom do I contact for leave issues? hr@novatech.example or the HR Helpdesk.

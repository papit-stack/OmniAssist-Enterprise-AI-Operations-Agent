PERFORMANCE REVIEW AND PROMOTIONS - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

Annual Performance Cycle
Performance reviews are held once a year (April) with a mid-year review in October. The review covers performance against goals, competencies, and team feedback.

How Goals Are Set
At the start of each cycle (April) employees and managers set 4-6 SMART goals aligned with team and company objectives. Goals are reviewed mid-year and may be updated.

Evaluation Method
Managers evaluate performance on a 5-point rating scale:
- 5 (Outstanding): Far exceeds expectations consistently.
- 4 (Exceeds): Exceeds expectations in most areas.
- 3 (Meets): Meets all expectations.
- 2 (Partially Meets): Meets some expectations.
- 1 (Does Not Meet): Below expectations.

Self-assessment and manager assessment are done on the portal, followed by the appraisal discussion meeting. Employees should bring their self-assessment notes and examples of work for discussion.

Outcomes of Ratings
- Rating 5: Highest bonus (see below) and top priority for promotion; named in the Excellence List.
- Rating 4: Above-average bonus; strong candidate for promotion.
- Rating 3: Standard bonus; normal growth trajectory.
- Rating 2: Reduced/no bonus; improvement plan is created.
- Rating 1: Improvement plan for 90 days; change of role or exit may follow.

Annual Bonus Calculation
The annual target bonus is 10% of annual CTC, paid in March, scaled by rating:
- Rating 5: 120% of target
- Rating 4: 100% of target
- Rating 3: 70% of target
- Rating 2: 25% of target
- Rating 1: 0% of target

Promotions
- Promotion reviews happen twice a year (April and October) along with the review cycle.
- Eligibility: minimum 18 months in current role at the time of review cycle (12 months for outstanding performers).
- Promotion is based on ratings (last 2 cycles average 4 or above), skills, and business need. A promotion committee approves all promotions.
- In the same cycle, an employee can be promoted only one level up. Promotions are effective from the 1st of the next month and come with a salary revision decided by HR and the manager.

Appeals
If you disagree with your rating, you may appeal to HR within 7 days of the rating communication. The appeal is reviewed by the department head and an HR representative.

Performance FAQ
- When is the appraisal? April every year, mid-year review in October.
- What is the bonus percentage? Target 10% of annual CTC, scaled by rating.
- Minimum tenure for promotion? 18 months in the current role, or 12 months for exceptional performers.
- How do I give self-assessment? Employee Portal > Appraisal > Self Assessment.

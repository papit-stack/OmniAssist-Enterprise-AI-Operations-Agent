EMPLOYEE BENEFITS AND PERKS - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

The following benefits are available to all permanent employees from the date of joining.

Health and Wellness
1. Group Medical Insurance: Cover of INR 8,00,000 per employee per year for self, spouse, and up to 2 dependent children. Provided through a top insurance partner. Employees can also voluntarily cover parents by paying a small premium.
2. Personal Accident Insurance: Cover of INR 15,00,000 for accidental death or disablement.
3. Group Term Life Insurance: Sum assured of INR 20,00,000 payable to the family in case of death during employment.
4. Annual Health Check-up: Free preventive health check-up once every year for all employees aged 30 years and above, and for all employees above 45.
5. Wellness Program: Free access to the wellness app "NovaCare" including mental health counseling sessions (up to 4 sessions per year), gym reimbursement of up to INR 5,000 per quarter, and quarterly health webinars.

Retirement and Financial Benefits
1. Provident Fund (PF): 12% of basic salary is contributed by the employee and an equal 12% is contributed by the company. Amount is deducted monthly from salary.
2. Gratuity: As per the Payment of Gratuity Act - eligible after 5 years of continuous service. Gratuity = Last drawn Basic + DA x 15 / 26 x number of years of service.
3. Professional Tax: Small monthly deduction as applicable per Karnataka state rules.
4. Tax-saving reimbursements: Medical reimbursement up to INR 15,000 per year and telephone reimbursement up to INR 20,000 per year against bills.

Learning and Growth Benefits
1. Learning budget: INR 25,000 per year per employee for courses, certifications, and books (see Learning & Development policy).
2. Certification bonus: INR 10,000 one-time bonus on completing select professional certifications.

Other Perks
1. Meal vouchers: Free lunch coupons on the days you work from office (up to INR 200 per working day).
2. Transport: Company cab service within a 15 km radius of the office for late-evening shifts ending after 9 PM.
3. Employee Referral Bonus: INR 40,000 for a successful referral who is hired and stays for 6 months.
4. Annual performance bonus: Paid as per the Performance Review policy, percentage decided on rating.
5. Work anniversary gift: A gift of employee's choice (merchandise or vouchers) on every work anniversary.

Benefits FAQ
- What is the medical insurance cover? INR 8,00,000 per year for self and dependents.
- Do I get a Provident Fund? Yes, 12% company contribution on top of your PF deduction.
- Is the premium waived for mental health counseling? Yes, up to 4 sessions per year are fully paid.
- How do I claim reimbursement? Submit invoices/bills from the Employee Portal > Reimbursements module.

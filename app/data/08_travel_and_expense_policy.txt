TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

Scope
This policy applies to business travel and office-related expenses incurred by employees. Claims are reimbursable only when pre-approved where applicable, supported with valid receipts, and claimed within 30 days.

Travel Booking
- Domestic and international travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal or approved agency. Self-booking is allowed only for amounts under INR 5,000 for transit (auto/cab) and only if cheaper.
- Flight bookings: Economy class is standard. Business class requires the approval of the department head.
- Hotel: 3-star or business hotels up to INR 4,500 per night (domestic). International hotels as per the country-specific limit provided in the portal.
- Trains/Air: For journeys below 800 km by rail, 2nd AC is permitted for managers and above; 3AC for others.

Per Diem and Daily Allowance
- Domestic travel: INR 900 per day toward food and incidental expenses. No receipt needed for per diem up to 6 hours (half-day) and full-day rules apply as per portal.
- International travel: The per diem differs by country and is published on the travel page.

Expenses Covered
- Air, rail, bus, and cab fares
- Hotel stay and boarding
- Meals beyond the per diem when a client meeting is involved (subject to approval)
- Courier charges for business documents
- Registration fees for business conferences (pre-approved)
- Internet/mobile data while travelling on assignment

Expenses Not Covered
- Personal expenses such as personal shopping, movies, alcoholic beverages, personal phone usage beyond the plan
- Fines (traffic/parking) incurred on personal vehicles
- Expenses without receipts
- Expenses claimed after 30 days without justification

Claim Process
1. Add expense entries in the Employee Portal > Reimbursements module, attaching digital receipts.
2. Submit the claim; it must be approved by the reporting manager and then Finance within 7 working days.
3. Approved claims are settled in the next payroll cycle or via direct transfer on a weekly basis, whichever is earlier.

Reimbursement Limits (Quick Reference)
- Cab within city for official work: approved daily or free pool usage.
- Medical reimbursement: INR 15,000/year.
- Telephone reimbursement: INR 20,000/year.
- Conference registration: pre-approved, no fixed cap.
- Client entertainment/dinner: INR 1,500 per person with advance approval from department head.

Advances
- Travel advance up to INR 50,000 can be requested for business trips, to be settled with bills within 15 days of return. Advances are deducted from salary if not settled.

Travel Expense FAQ
- How much is the daily allowance for domestic travel? INR 900 per day.
- Can I claim personal cab fares? No, only business-related transport is payable.
- What if I lose a receipt? Submit an affidavit; claims above INR 2,000 without receipt need department head approval.
- Whom to contact for expense issues? finance@novatech.example.

OFFICE AND FACILITIES - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

Office Locations and Addresses
1. Head Office (Bengaluru): No. 12, Tech Park Road, Whitefield, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560066. GF and 1st Floor, Building B.
2. Pune Office: 5th Floor, Vertex Towers, Baner Road, Pune, Maharashtra 411045.
3. Hyderabad Office: 3rd Floor, Cyber Heights, HITEC City, Madhapur, Hyderabad, Telangana 500081.

Office Timings and Access
- Standard office hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Offices remain open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM for flexi timing with manager approval.
- Office access is through the employee ID card. Cards are activated on joining and deactivated on exit. Lost cards can be re-issued by Admin (INR 200 charge).
- Visitor entry is via the front desk; visitors must sign in and wear a visitor badge.

Facilities Available at All Offices
1. Cafeteria: subsidized meals and a canteen; lunch coupon worth INR 200 issued on WFO days (see Benefits).
2. Cafeterias/Kitchens: Coffee, tea, and snacks are available free on each floor.
3. Meeting Rooms: Book through the calendar system up to 2 weeks in advance; video-conferencing enabled.
4. Cab Service: Late-evening cab within 15 km radius for employees ending after 9 PM; app-based booking on the portal.
5. Parking: Free two-wheeler and car parking for employees; vehicle sticker from Admin required.
6. Wellness Room: A quiet/first-aid room is available on the ground floor.
7. Gym: On-premises gym at Bengaluru office, open 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM; gym reimbursement of INR 5,000/quarter for other offices (see Benefits).
8. WiFi: Guest WiFi available for visitors; employees use secure corporate network.
9. IT Helpdesk: Located on the ground floor; ticket system via ithelpdesk@novatech.example.

Housekeeping and Admin
- For maintenance requests (AC, lights, furniture), raise a ticket on the Admin module of the portal.
- Stationery: available from the Admin counter on each floor.
- Desk allocation: fixed desks in most teams; hot-desking in project pods.

Emergency Information
- Fire exits are clearly marked; evacuation drill held once every 6 months.
- Emergency contact: Security desk at extension 911, or 100/112 for emergencies.
- First-aid kits and AED are located at the reception and wellness room.

Facilities FAQ
- What are the office timings? 9 AM to 6 PM, flexi between 8 AM and 8 PM.
- How do I book a meeting room? Through the calendar booking system, up to 2 weeks in advance.
- What do I do if my desk equipment is faulty? Raise a ticket with IT Helpdesk.
- Can I get a cab at night? Yes, if you end work after 9 PM and live within 15 km.

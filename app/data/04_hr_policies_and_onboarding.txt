HR POLICIES AND ONBOARDING - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM with a one-hour lunch break between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM. Lunch breaks are flexible with team arrangement. Overtime is not a practice, however critical project work may require extra hours which will be compensated with comp-off (see Leave Policy).

Attendance
Attendance is marked daily through the biometric system or the mobile attendance app when working from home. Employees must mark attendance on entering and leaving the office. Late arrival beyond 15 minutes is recorded as half-day casual leave after 3 late punches in a calendar month.

Hybrid Work Model
With effect from 2023, employees work from the office on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, and work from home on Tuesday and Thursday. Remote work days can be swapped with manager approval. Permanent work-from-home is considered only in exceptional cases such as disability or relocation with HR approval.

Probation Period
Every new employee has a probation period of 6 months, extendable by a maximum of 3 months. During probation, regular feedback is given. Confirmation is subject to satisfactory performance. No leave encashment is applicable during probation.

Notice Period and Resignation
- Notice period for regular employees: 60 days, or buy-out at management discretion.
- Notice period during probation: 15 days.
- Unauthorized absence of 8 consecutive days without intimation is treated as abandonment of employment.

Code of Conduct
Employees must maintain professionalism, honesty, and respect in the workplace. Bullying, harassment, discrimination, and any violation of equity are strictly prohibited and will lead to disciplinary action. Details are available in the full Code of Conduct document on the portal. Report any concern to hr@novatech.example or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline.

Background Verification (BGV)
All offers are subject to successful background verification, which is initiated before the joining date.

Onboarding Process (First 90 Days)
Day 1: You will receive your laptop, ID card, email account, and other access. Attend the HR induction session explaining policies, benefits, and portal usage.
Week 1: Meet your manager and team, complete system access setup, and complete mandatory trainings on data security, POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment), and workplace ethics.
Month 1: Onboarding buddy is assigned. First 30-60-90 day goals are discussed. You will receive your joining kit and access to all internal tools.
Month 3: First performance feedback session.

Documents Required on Joining
- Proof of identity (Aadhaar/Passport/PAN)
- Educational certificates (final degree)
- Address proof
- Previous employer relieving letter and last 3 months payslips
- Bank account details for salary
- A recent passport-size photograph

HR FAQ
- What is the probation period? 6 months.
- How long is the notice period? 60 days for confirmed employees, 15 days during probation.
- How do I take a WFH day? Swap your schedule with your manager's approval through the portal.
- Whom do I contact for HR queries? hr@novatech.example or HR Helpdesk at extension 500.

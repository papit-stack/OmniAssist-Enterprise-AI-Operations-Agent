REMOTE WORK POLICY

Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for employees who work remotely or use a hybrid work arrangement.

Eligibility

Remote work eligibility depends on:

Job responsibilities
Team requirements
Business needs
Manager approval
Employee performance and reliability

Not all roles are suitable for remote work.

Working Hours

Employees working remotely are expected to maintain their agreed working schedule and remain available during required core working hours.

Employees should communicate any significant changes to their availability with their manager.

Availability and Communication

Employees must remain reachable through approved company communication channels during working hours.

Employees are expected to:

Attend scheduled meetings.
Respond to important messages within a reasonable timeframe.
Keep their calendar and availability reasonably up to date.
Inform their manager of extended periods of unavailability.
Work Environment

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable workspace that supports productive and professional work.

The workspace should provide reasonable privacy and minimize interruptions during meetings and confidential discussions.

Company Equipment

Company-issued equipment must be used responsibly and protected from unauthorized access.

Employees should not allow unauthorized individuals to use company devices or access company systems.

Information Security

Employees working remotely must follow all company security requirements.

Employees must:

Use approved company accounts and applications.
Protect passwords and authentication credentials.
Avoid sharing confidential company information through unauthorized services.
Use secure internet connections when accessing company systems.
Report suspected security incidents promptly.
Meetings

Employees are expected to attend required meetings and maintain professional standards during virtual meetings.

If an employee cannot attend a required meeting, they should notify the meeting organizer or manager in advance when possible.

Remote Work Location

Employees should work from an approved location unless their manager has authorized an alternative arrangement.

Working from another country or location may require additional approval due to legal, tax, security, or compliance requirements.

Performance

Remote employees are subject to the same performance expectations as employees working from the office.

Performance is evaluated based on responsibilities, results, collaboration, communication, and other applicable performance criteria.

Policy Compliance

Failure to follow this policy may result in changes to remote work privileges or other appropriate management action.

The company may modify or suspend remote work arrangements based on business requirements.

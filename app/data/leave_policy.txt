LEAVE POLICY

Purpose

This policy defines the rules and procedures for requesting, approving, and managing employee leave.

Types of Leave

Employees may request the following types of leave:

Annual Leave: Paid time off for personal or vacation purposes.
Sick Leave: Leave taken when an employee is ill or requires medical attention.
Emergency Leave: Short-notice leave required due to an unexpected personal or family emergency.
Bereavement Leave: Leave provided following the death of an immediate family member.
Unpaid Leave: Leave that may be approved when an employee has exhausted applicable paid leave or requires an extended absence.
Annual Leave

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 3 working days before the intended start date.

Annual leave is subject to manager approval and business requirements.

Employees should avoid scheduling leave during critical project deadlines unless prior approval has been obtained.

Sick Leave

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible when they are unable to work due to illness.

For extended periods of illness, the company may request appropriate medical documentation in accordance with applicable law and company procedures.

Emergency Leave

Emergency leave may be requested when an unexpected situation requires the employee's immediate attention.

Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible and provide an expected return date when known.

Leave Approval

Submitting a leave request does not guarantee approval.

Managers consider:

Team workload
Project deadlines
Existing approved leave
Business continuity
The employee's available leave balance

Employees should wait for approval before making commitments that depend on the leave being granted.

Leave Cancellation

An employee may request cancellation of previously approved leave by notifying their manager.

Cancellation is subject to operational requirements and manager approval.

Leave Records

Employees are responsible for reviewing their leave balances and ensuring that submitted requests contain accurate dates and information.

The company maintains official leave records through its designated HR system.

Policy Compliance

Employees are expected to follow this policy and any applicable employment laws and local regulations.

The company reserves the right to update this policy when business or legal requirements change.

COMPANY OVERVIEW - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

About the Company
NovaTech Solutions Private Limited (NovaTech) is a technology company founded in 2014. We design and build software products and provide IT services. Today the company has more than 1,200 employees and serves clients across banking, healthcare, and retail industries.

Mission and Vision
Mission: To build reliable software that makes everyday business simple.
Vision: To be the most trusted technology partner for companies worldwide.

Core Values
1. Customer first - We prioritize client needs and outcomes.
2. Integrity - We act honestly and transparently in everything we do.
3. Innovation - We encourage new ideas and continuous improvement.
4. Collaboration - We succeed together as one team.
5. Ownership - We take responsibility for our work and results.

Company Structure
The company is divided into the following departments:
- Product & Engineering
- Sales & Marketing
- Client Services / Support
- Human Resources (HR)
- Finance & Accounts
- IT & Infrastructure
- Administration

The Leadership Team includes the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Chief Operating Officer (COO), Head of Sales, Head of HR, and Head of Finance. Each department has a department head who reports to the leadership team.

Offices and Locations
Head Office: No. 12, Tech Park Road, Whitefield, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560066, India.
Branch Offices: Pune and Hyderabad, India.
With effect from 2023, NovaTech operates a hybrid work model. Most teams work from the office on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday and work from home on Tuesday and Thursday.

Company Size and Background
Employees: approximately 1,200 as of 2025.
Revenue: annual revenue of approximately USD 45 million.
Founded: 2014 by two co-founders. Profitable since 2018.
Employer of Choice: listed among the "Best IT Workplaces" in India for 2024.

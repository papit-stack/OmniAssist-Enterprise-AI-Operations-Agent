EXPENSE POLICY

Purpose

This policy defines the rules for submitting, approving, and reimbursing legitimate business expenses incurred by employees.

Eligible Expenses

Reasonable and necessary expenses incurred for legitimate business purposes may be reimbursable.

Examples include:

Business travel
Transportation
Accommodation
Business meals
Client or business meetings
Approved conferences and events
Other expenses specifically authorized by the company
Prior Approval

Employees should obtain manager approval before incurring significant or unusual business expenses.

Travel and other expenses that exceed department-approved limits may require additional authorization.

Business Travel

Employees should use reasonable and cost-effective transportation and accommodation options.

When booking travel, employees should use company-approved travel providers when available.

Employees are expected to choose reasonable options based on business requirements rather than personal preference.

Meals

Business meals may be reimbursed when they are directly related to legitimate business activities.

Expense claims should identify the business purpose and, where applicable, the participants.

Personal meals that are not permitted under company policy are not reimbursable.

Receipts

Employees must retain receipts or other required supporting documentation for reimbursable expenses.

Receipts should clearly show:

Date
Vendor
Amount
Description of the expense

If a receipt is unavailable, the employee should follow the company's approved missing-receipt procedure.

Expense Submission

Employees should submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense unless their department has a different deadline.

Each expense claim should include:

Expense date
Amount
Currency
Business purpose
Appropriate category
Supporting receipt or documentation
Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are generally not reimbursable unless specifically approved:

Personal purchases
Fines or penalties
Personal entertainment
Alcohol purchased for personal consumption
Expenses unrelated to company business
Upgrades made for personal preference
Expenses that violate company policy
Approval

Expense claims must be reviewed and approved by the appropriate manager or designated approver.

Approval confirms that the expense is business-related, reasonable, and supported by appropriate documentation.

Reimbursement

Approved expenses will be reimbursed through the company's designated payment or payroll process.

Processing times may vary depending on the company's finance procedures.

Policy Violations

Submitting false, altered, duplicate, or intentionally misleading expense claims may result in disciplinary action.

The company may request additional documentation or clarification before approving an expense.

Policy Compliance

Employees are responsible for understanding and following this policy.

The company reserves the right to update expense limits, approval requirements, and reimbursement procedures as business or legal requirements change.

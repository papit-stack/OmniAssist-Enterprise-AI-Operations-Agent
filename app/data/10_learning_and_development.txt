LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT - NovaTech Solutions Private Limited

Learning is a core benefit at NovaTech. Every employee gets time and budget to grow skills.

Learning Budget
- INR 25,000 per year for every employee on courses, books, certifications, and conferences.
- The budget covers the full cost of work-related training. Unused budget does not carry forward.
- Courses must be approved by the manager. Requests are made through Employee Portal > Learning > Course Request.

Training Hours and Learning Days
- Every employee is expected to complete at least 5 days (40 hours) of learning per year.
- Learning days are treated as working days; no leave deduction for approved training.
- Manager-approved study time during business hours of up to 4 hours per week is available.

Mandatory Trainings
Every employee must complete the following annual e-learnings in the LMS:
1. Data & Cyber Security Awareness
2. POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) awareness
3. Workplace Ethics and Code of Conduct
4. Intellectual Property basics
Completion is tracked and reflected in the annual review.

Technical and Functional Training
- Cloud certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP) - company sponsors 100% cost and gives 1 extra study day per exam.
- Professional certifications (e.g., PMP, Agile/Scrum, CISSP) - 100% sponsorship and one-time bonus of INR 10,000 on completion.
- Internal bootcamps on new technologies are run quarterly by the Engineering Academy.
- Soft-skills workshops (communication, leadership, time management) are conducted every quarter via an external partner.

Career Growth Framework
- Career tracks: Individual Contributor (IC) and Manager tracks; technical roles have parallel growth (Engineer -> Senior -> Lead -> Architect -> Principal).
- Career roadmap discussions happen during the mid-year and annual reviews.
- Internal job postings are advertised first before external hiring; employees can apply after 18 months in their current role (with manager approval).

Mentorship Program
- Every employee under 2 years of experience is assigned a mentor from a senior level.
- Mentor meetings are recommended once every 2 weeks at 30 minutes.
- Contact the L&D team at lnd@novatech.example to request a mentor or become one.

Learning FAQ
- How much is my training budget? INR 25,000 per year.
- Do I need manager approval for a course? Yes, courses need manager approval on the portal.
- Is the certification exam fee reimbursed? Yes, 100% for approved work-related certifications.
- Can I attend a conference abroad? Yes, with department head approval; travel covered as per Travel policy.
